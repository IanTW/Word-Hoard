**Status: satisfied, not lifted.** The six-step slice was completed 2026-08-29,
and on 2026-09-08 the user confirmed they are studying with it. The gate this
memory described has opened: work after the slice (M1 to M8 in `docs/TODO.md`)
may now proceed in its planned order.

The original rule: one working slice end to end before scope widened in any
direction (database, a few dozen hand-entered German words, FSRS with learning
steps, the typing exercise, the review loop, a minimal due-queue interface).
Nothing else until step 6 worked and had real review sessions behind it.

**Why:** the risk was building four exercise types, a content pipeline and a
multi-language abstraction before finding the core loop was wrong. It paid off
twice: the first session's 9 Again out of 17 showed the app demanded words it
had never taught, and the first daily use on 2026-09-10 produced the M2a request
(finish and go again) rather than a guessed feature.

**How to apply:** the spirit survives the gate. Prefer changes that real use
has asked for; for open questions (retention 0.95, ten new a day, the missing
Easy rating, the Go again grant size) wait for evidence rather than argue.
Still do not build a per-language processing abstraction until Dutch content
actually exists, because the points of variation cannot be seen from one
language. See [[no-gamification]] and [[user-data-is-disposable-for-now]].
